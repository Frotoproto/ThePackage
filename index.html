01 — About

A single editor for the whole release, not a rotation of freelancers.

Most creators stitch together five different editors, two thumbnail artists, and a motion designer they found through a referral. The result is inconsistent pacing, mismatched colour, and a release that always slips by a week.

ThePackage replaces that with one person, one workflow, and one rate. You send the raw cut, the brief, and the deadline. You get back a finished edit, motion graphic, or thumbnail that matches the previous one — every time.
