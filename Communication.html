02 — What to send

A useful brief is short, concrete, and includes the deadline.

Platform. YouTube, TikTok, Reels, broadcast, internal? Output ratios and codec requirements can change the approach.

Deadline. The day you need the master — not the day you’d like to start. Turnaround is usually 48 hours for short form and 3–5 days for long form.

References. One or two links to work you want it to feel like. Anything else is welcome but not required.
